Sequencing Weights Source
Optimal scoring weights are derived from the Monte Carlo simulation manuscript. FFCF: ROCE 38.5%, Budget penalty 61.5%. EBIT Max: ROCE 42.5%, NPV 24.1%, IRR 22.5%, Budget 10.9%. Override in Sequencing tab.

Metric Definitions

NPV — All future cash flows discounted at WACC
IRR — Rate that makes NPV = 0; compare to WACC as hurdle
ROCE — 10-yr cumulative EBIT ÷ budget (un-discounted multiple)
Cap Efficiency — NPV ÷ Budget (value per dollar invested)
Payback — Year when cumulative FCF turns positive
Score — Composite index: w·NPV + w·IRR + w·ROCE − w·Budget
Terminal Value — EBIT × multiple added at horizon end
